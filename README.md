# A Material Point Method Study of the Effects of Material Heterogeneity on the Statistical Characteristics of Continuous Icebreaking Loads and Fracture Behavior

## Overview

This repository contains the source code for the study titled:

**A Material Point Method Study of the Effects of Material Heterogeneity on the Statistical Characteristics of Continuous Icebreaking Loads and Fracture Behavior**

This study investigates the effects of material heterogeneity on the statistical characteristics of continuous icebreaking loads and fracture behavior using the Material Point Method.
